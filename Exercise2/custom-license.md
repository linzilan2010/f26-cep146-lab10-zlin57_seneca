# Exercise 2: Build Your Own License

## Emotion Recognition AI - Custom License

**Creators:** Zilan Lin and Lorenzo

**Project:** Emotion Recognition AI  
**Version:** 1.0

### 1. Who Can Use the Model?
Anyone, including students, researchers, developers, and companies, can use our AI model.

### 2. Permissions
Users are allowed to:
- Use the model for personal, educational, and commercial purposes.
- Modify and improve the model.
- Redistribute the original or modified model.

### 3. Requirements
Users must:
- Give credit to the original creators.
- Include this license when redistributing the model.
- Clearly state any modifications they make.
- Share the source code of modified versions when distributing them.

### 4. Restrictions
Users must not:
- Claim ownership of the original model.
- Use the model for illegal activities.
- Collect personal information without appropriate consent.
- Remove the original copyright or attribution notices.

### 5. License Violations
If users violate these terms, their permission to use and distribute the model will be terminated. Permission may be restored if the violation is corrected and the creators agree.

### 6. Disclaimer
The model is provided "as is," without any warranty. The creators are not responsible for errors or misuse of the model.

---

## Explanation of Our License Choices

We chose this license because we want our AI model to be accessible to everyone. Allowing commercial use and modification encourages innovation and learning. We require attribution to protect the original creators and sharing improvements to help other developers. We also added restrictions to encourage responsible and ethical use of AI.

Our license balances flexibility with responsibility. It allows people to build new applications while respecting the original work.
